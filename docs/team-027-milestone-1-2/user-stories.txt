Problem statement: Option 2, Student Mentoring and Career Support Platform

================================================================================
3. User Stories
================================================================================

The stories below come from the user research pain points. Each one names a single
user and action (Specific), includes a number or limit that can be tested
(Measurable), fits the course timeline (Achievable), links to a pain point
(Relevant), and states a time limit where timing matters (Time-bound).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Epic 1: Student Needs and Mentor Discovery
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Story 1.1: Record a Mentoring Need
As a student, I want to record my need by choosing a pathway (job, higher studies,
venture or unsure) and domain and writing a specific question of at least 80
characters, so that the right mentor can be found for my situation.
Addresses pain points: 1, 3, 4

Story 1.2: Browse and Filter Mentors
As a student, I want to filter verified mentor profiles by domain, current role,
company and career path, so that I can find mentors whose experience matches my goal.
Addresses pain points: 1, 3

Story 1.3: Submit a Mentorship Request
As a student, I want to submit a request for my needs, naming up to three preferred
mentors, with at most three open requests at a time, so that I no longer have to
cold-message strangers.
Addresses pain points: 2, 4

Story 1.4: Track Request Status
As a student, I want to be notified within 5 minutes whenever my request changes
status (Submitted, Under review, Matched, Declined or Expired, which happens after
7 days without a decision), so that I know whether to wait or look elsewhere.
Addresses pain point: 2

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Epic 2: Mentor Profile and Capacity
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Story 2.1: Mentor Profile
As a mentor, I want to list my domain, current role, company, career path and the
topics I can help with, kept hidden from students until an administrator verifies me,
so that I am matched only with students whose needs fit my experience.
Addresses pain points: 1, 3

Story 2.2: Capacity and Pause
As a mentor, I want to set how many mentees I take at a time (default one) and pause
new requests, so that I am shown as "Full" and receive no new requests, so that
mentoring fits the 2–3 hours I can realistically give.
Addresses pain points: 4, 5

Story 2.3: Review Before Accepting
As a mentor, I want to read a student’s need and question and accept, or decline
with a reason, within 7 days, so that I only take on requests I can genuinely
help with.
Addresses pain point: 4

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Epic 3: Matching and Administrator Approval
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Story 3.1: Mentor Recommendations
As a program administrator, I want the system to recommend the top five verified
mentors with spare capacity for each new request, ranked by domain match and showing
the reasons, so that I no longer match students manually.
Addresses pain points: 1, 7

Story 3.2: Confirm Availability and Approve
As a program administrator, I want to send the mentor an availability check and
approve or reassign the pairing before the student and mentor are introduced, so
that students are not assigned to mentors who are no longer available.
Addresses pain points: 5, 7

Story 3.3: Mentor Verification
As a program administrator, I want to verify a mentor’s roll number and graduation
year against the program’s graduate list before their profile goes live, so that
students can trust the people they contact.
Addresses pain point: 9

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Epic 4: Mentoring Journey
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Story 4.1: Shared Goals
As a student, I want to agree two or three goals with my mentor, each with a target
date, and mark them done, so that our mentoring stays focused alongside my coursework.
Addresses pain points: 3, 6

Story 4.2: Private Session Log
As a student or mentor, I want to log each session’s date, duration and status
(held, cancelled or no-show) with no field for what was discussed, so that our
progress is tracked while our conversation stays private.
Addresses pain points: 6, 9

Story 4.3: Mentee Overview
As a mentor, I want to see all my mentees with their goal progress and next
session date in one list, so that I no longer mix students up across WhatsApp and
LinkedIn.
Addresses pain point: 6

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Epic 5: Feedback, Outcomes and Insights
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Story 5.1: Closing Feedback
As a student or mentor, I want to give a 1–5 rating and an optional comment when
a mentorship closes, with the mentor able to see the student’s rating, so that
mentors learn whether their guidance helped.
Addresses pain point: 8

Story 5.2: Record Outcomes
As a student, I want to record outcomes such as an interview cleared or an
internship secured, each with a date, so that the impact of mentoring is captured.
Addresses pain point: 8

Story 5.3: Inactivity Alerts
As a program administrator, I want to be alerted when an active pair has logged
no session for 14 days, so that I can follow up or reassign the student.
Addresses pain point: 6

Story 5.4: Program Dashboard
As a program administrator, I want a dashboard for each term showing requests,
approved pairs, sessions per pair, completion rate, average rating and outcomes,
with no conversation content and an option to export as CSV, so that I can show
the institute whether mentoring is working.
Addresses pain points: 8, 9

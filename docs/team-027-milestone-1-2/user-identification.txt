Problem statement: Option 2, Student Mentoring and Career Support Platform

Objective:
To help IITM BS students find, reach and keep learning from alumni and recent graduates who have walked a similar career path. The platform does this through structured mentoring requests, administrator-approved matching, goal and session tracking, and outcome reporting that never exposes private conversations.

================================================================================
1. Identification of Users
================================================================================

We classify users by whom the platform is built for. Students are the people the platform exists to help. Mentors use it to deliver that help. Administrative users run and oversee the program but are not the ones being mentored.

S.No | Primary Users                          | Secondary Users                       | Tertiary Users
-----+----------------------------------------+---------------------------------------+---------------------------------------
1    | Students approaching graduation        | Alumni and recent graduates           | IIC / Placement Cell administrator
     | (mentees)                              | (mentors)                             |

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User Descriptions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Primary Users: Students (mentees)
   Students approaching graduation who need guidance on career direction, skills
   to prioritize, portfolio projects, internships, company interview processes or
   higher studies. They record their needs, request mentors, agree goals with
   them and give feedback.

2. Secondary Users: Alumni and recent graduates (mentors)
   IITM BS graduates working in industry, studying further or running ventures.
   They volunteer for a limited time (about 2–3 hours per month, usually one
   mentee at a time) and use the platform to set their capacity, review requests
   and log sessions.

3. Tertiary Users: IIC / Placement Cell administrator
   Verifies mentors, reviews system-recommended matches, reconfirms mentor
   availability, approves or reassigns pairings, monitors engagement and handles
   reported issues.

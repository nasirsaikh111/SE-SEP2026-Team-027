Application: Sangam: Student Mentoring and Career Support Platform
Team: Team 027 · Rituparna (Product Manager), Hemant (TL/Frontend), Payal (Tester), Debshata (Scrum Master), Nasir (Backend)
Problem statement: Option 2, Student Mentoring and Career Support Platform

Objective:
To help IITM BS students find, reach and keep learning from alumni and recent
graduates who have walked a similar career path. The platform does this through
structured mentoring requests, administrator-approved matching, goal and session
tracking, and outcome reporting that never exposes private conversations.

================================================================================
1. Problem Statement
================================================================================
IITM BS degree students approaching graduation lack a structured, trustworthy
channel to find, reach, and learn from alumni mentors. Career advice comes from
scattered, unverified sources (YouTube, peer groups, LinkedIn). Students
hesitate to cold-message seniors, and when they do, messages often go
unanswered. Mentors are overwhelmed by vague "please guide me" requests and
repetitive questions. The IIC/Placement Cell has no system-supported process
for matching, tracking, or measuring mentoring outcomes.

Key insight: The problem is not a shortage of willing mentors. It is the difficulty
of discovering, reaching and staying connected with the right person at the right
time, without overloading volunteers or compromising privacy.

================================================================================
2. Key Pain Points Identified (from 10 Qualitative Interviews)
================================================================================
1. Finding a relevant mentor is hard:
   Students struggle to find someone with the right background; no system support
   for matching mentors to specialized needs.
2. Cold outreach goes unanswered, and students hesitate to ask:
   Messages to seniors on LinkedIn get mixed responses; students fear questions
   are too basic.
3. Students lack practical, specific guidance:
   General advice exists, but students cannot find out company-specific interview
   rounds or skill priorities.
4. Vague, unstructured requests overload mentors:
   Mentors receive open-ended "please guide me" asks and repetitive questions.
5. Mentor time is limited and availability changes:
   Volunteers have limited hours (2-3 hrs/month), and availability goes out of date.
6. Mentoring rarely continues beyond one conversation:
   Communication breaks after a single chat without shared records or goals.
7. There is no formal mentoring process or channel:
   Informal outreach across WhatsApp/LinkedIn with no formal institutional workflow.
8. Nobody can see whether mentoring works:
   No visibility into meeting counts, goal progress, or tangible outcomes.
9. Trust and privacy are hard to balance:
   Mentors cannot verify student identities easily, and program oversight must
   never read private conversations.

================================================================================
3. Core System Features
================================================================================
F1. Accounts & Access (Role-Based JWT Authentication)
    - Three distinct roles: Student (Mentee), Mentor (Alumni), and IIC Admin.
    - Verified IITM email domain check for student registrations.
    - IIC approves students and verifies alumni roll numbers against graduate records.

F2. Discover Mentors Directory
    - Curated directory of verified alumni with domain, role, company, and skills.
    - Student can name a preferred mentor (advisory preference, no auto-pairing).
    - Contact details (email/phone) remain hidden until IIC match approval.

F3. Structured Mentoring Request Lifecycle
    - Student records need with pathway (Job, Higher Studies, Venture, Unsure),
      domain, background, and a specific problem statement (80+ characters).
    - Enforced constraint: One open request per student at a time.
    - Status lifecycle: SUBMITTED -> UNDER_REVIEW -> WAITING -> MATCHED / CLOSED.

F4. Administrator-Approved Matching & Availability Checks
    - System suggests ranked mentor candidates with match scores and explanations.
    - IIC sends availability check to shortlisted mentor (7-day response window).
    - Match activates only after mentor confirms and IIC officially approves.

F5. Mentorship Workspace (Goals, Sessions & Privacy-First Telemetry)
    - 1 to 3 agreed goals per mentorship with target dates and progress tracking.
    - Private meeting logging: session date, duration (minutes), and status only.
    - Zero chat transcript storage; conversations remain strictly private.

F6. Scheduling & Google Calendar Integration
    - Mentors connect Google Calendar via OAuth to schedule sessions.
    - Auto-generated Google Meet video conference links.

F7. Feedback, Outcomes & Program Monitoring
    - Bi-weekly mentor feedback to IIC; closing 1-5 rating and qualitative review.
    - Tangible outcome tracking: interviews cleared, internships secured, job offers.
    - 14-day inactivity alerts and CSV export for institutional reporting.

F8. Advisory AI Assistant & Knowledge Base
    - Rule-based mentor suggestions and request priority triage (advisory to IIC).
    - "Ask Sangam" helpdesk chatbot grounded strictly in published IIC articles.
